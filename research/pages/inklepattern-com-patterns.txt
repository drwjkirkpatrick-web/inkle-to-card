# Free inkle loom and band patterns you can edit

Choose a plain-weave color order or a Baltic pickup starter. Open it in the [homepage generator](https://inklepattern.com/#tool) to edit the draft and download a loom-side sheet.

Plain weave uses a numbered heddled/open (H/U) warp order and alternating sheds. Baltic pickup adds pattern-thread choices for each row; use its pick/drop chart rather than treating the motif as a stripe sequence.

Want to design from a blank draft? [Start with the generator](https://inklepattern.com/#tool), then return here when you want an editable starter.

**Choose your technique:** [8 plain-weave starters](https://inklepattern.com/patterns/#plainWeaveCatalog) and [4 Baltic pickup starters](https://inklepattern.com/patterns/#pickupCatalog). Tablet weaving needs a different draft and is not supported here.

Prefer a ready-to-print sheet? [Download the free 2-page starter PDF](https://inklepattern.com/assets/two-17-end-inkle-starters.2eb7aad7cd0a.pdf) with Quiet Harbor plain weave plus Bordered Pickup Key Fob on Letter paper, no account needed ( [See what is included](https://inklepattern.com/patterns/#starter-worksheet)). Use the worksheet as-is, or open a starter in the generator to edit colors and print your own sheet.

Actual adjustments for this sampling setup

Alternate the natural shed: H upper for odd rows, U upper for even rows. Pattern ends already in the required layer stay there. Weave a short plain-weave header and finish before removing the sample from the loom.
