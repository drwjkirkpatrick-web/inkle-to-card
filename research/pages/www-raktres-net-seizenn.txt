See [SeiZenn-Interface.pdf](https://www.raktres.net/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/SeiZenn-Interface.pdf) and [SeiZenn-Create-your-first-pattern.pdf](https://www.raktres.net/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/SeiZenn-Create-your-first-pattern.pdf) for a short introduction to SeiZenn.

## Tools

Different modules are available:

[to draw a pattern as you weave](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/inkle "to draw a pattern as you weave") Edit an inkle loom pattern

[to draw pattern on a grid](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/grid "to draw pattern on a grid") Edit a square pattern

[to draw heddle loom pattern, potholder](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/heddle "to draw heddle loom pattern, potholder") Edit your pattern for shaft loom, heddle loom, potholder

[to draw tabletweaving pattern](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/tablet "to draw tabletweaving pattern") Edit your tablet pattern

[to draw pattern for disk weaving](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/disk "to draw pattern for disk weaving") Edit pattern for disk loom

[Takadai pattern](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/takadai "Takadai pattern") Edit pattern for takadai

[Create your palette](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/palette "Create your palette") Edit your color palette

[List of your patterns](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/list "List of your patterns") List of your patterns

[Login](https://www.raktres.net/seizenn/#/account "Login") Your personnal page

[Blog](https://www.raktres.net/blog/ "Blog") Check the blog to get news or post a comment

The interface is a little different on a mobile device (tablet or phone, indicated by the icon  top right), or on a computer (icon ).

On a mobile device, the buttons are icons. It is possible to place them to the left or above by checking the box .

On a computer, the buttons are by default textual as for the first version. To use icons, check the box  top right.

### Terms of service

Seizenn is a free to use service for non-commercial use.

If you want to use it beyond your personal use, for example to illustrate a book, [please contact me](https://www.raktres.net/blog/contact/). I am very interested to know if you use it during a workshop, to follow the use which is made of it.

This tool is offered without guarantee. The anomalies that you wish to report will be studied with kindness, without any promise of correction delay.

The patterns you save can be lost, and there is no special technical protection to prevent someone from accessing them.
