## Description

## _Choose from five styles of card weaving cards:_

LACIS:

25 laminated plastic card weaving cards, .019″ thick, 2-1/2″ square. For card and tablet weaving. Virtually indestructible, will not tear while providing a smooth surface for manipulation of threads.

SCHACHT:

25 cardboard cards, 3.25″ square. Holes are 1-5/8th” apart on center.

Each side of the card is a different color, so from the side view you can easily see if any of your cards are not aligned properly with the others.

HENDRICKSON 4 hole:

Pack of 25 cards These Tablet Weaving Cards are made of cardboard, and are 3.5″ on each side. Sides are marked A, B, C and D to help with keeping order while weaving.
